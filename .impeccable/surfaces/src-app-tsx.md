Scope: the single-page wedding invitation (src/App.tsx and src/components/*). Mode: Experience (the invitation itself leads). Audience: family and friends opening a WhatsApp link on a phone. Action: remember the date, get directions to R.C. Mahal. Constraint: every word of existing copy frozen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Entering a Tamil wedding mandapam, told as an unrolled Kanchipuram silk saree: carved mandapam doors part, then each section is a silk panel bounded by gold zari temple borders. Refuses the cream-card wedding template with identical icon cards and tracked eyebrows.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory silk body (#FFF8EC with woven noise), deep maroon and burgundy silk panels (#641C2B/#781F35), antique-gold zari (#C49A45 to #E8D5A3 foil line work), blush (#F5DDE0) only in rose petals and portrait mats, deep green (#234B38) only from real mango and banana leaves. Gold engraved line art for pillars, arch, kuthu vilakku, Nadaswaram, Thavil. Realistic jasmine strings and canvas-rendered rose petals. Type: Rozha One names, Cinzel inscriptional caps, EB Garamond italic voice, Jost small info, Noto Serif Tamil.

STORY: Doors open; guest sees the couple's names under a gold mandapam arch hung with toran and jasmine, then meets the couple, learns date, Muhurtham time and venue, watches the countdown, browses photos, taps Get Directions, receives the blessing close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height ivory mandapam. Realistic marigold-mango toran across the top, jasmine strings at both edges, gold pillars and banana leaves framing the sides, a gold arch around centred text: Tamil heading, SAVE THE DATE, names at display scale, date, time, tagline, venue. Two lit kuthu vilakku at the base. No primary button in hero; directions sit in Venue.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). "Create a premium visual identity inspired by traditional Tamil and South Indian weddings... The visitor should feel as though they are entering a beautifully decorated traditional wedding mandapam." Signature interaction: door-opening entrance; scroll pushes pillars and leaves outward as if walking through the entrance; petals drift at depth-scaled scroll speeds.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
